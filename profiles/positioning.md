### How We're Different

Unlike reactive freelancers who wait for your panicked message or overpriced agencies that bill you for every hour of chaos, DigiSavvy watches your systems proactively at a flat monthly rate with no surprises. That means your problems get solved before they become emergencies — and you stop paying the hidden tax of constant firefighting.

### Why It Works

Bootstrapped founders did not start their business to become IT managers, and the fear that technology will always steal their time and attention is both real and deeply personal. This positioning speaks directly to that frustration and offers the one thing most tech support never does: peace of mind before the crisis hits.

## Objection Handling

**"Aren't you limiting yourself by being so specific?"**
"Actually, being specific is exactly what makes us valuable. Any generalist tech support shop can react to a broken site. Very few are watching proactively for bootstrapped online founders at a flat rate. When the right founder finds us, they do not need to be convinced — they already know we are built for them. That clarity is worth more than a wider net."

**"How is this different from hiring a freelancer?"**
"A freelancer waits for your message. We are already watching. The difference is not just availability — it is posture. Reactive support means you are always the one who notices the problem first. Proactive support means we often solve it before you ever knew it existed. Add flat rate pricing to that and you have eliminated two of the biggest frustrations founders have with traditional tech support: the chaos and the unpredictable invoices."

**"Why should I believe this will work for me?"**
"Fair question. The founders we work with typically come to us after years of duct-taping their tech together themselves or cycling through freelancers who were never really watching. What changes is not just the support — it is the clarity. You know what is covered, you know what you are paying, and you have someone who actually picks up the phone. The best way to test that is to start with a free systems review. No commitment, just a clear picture of where your fires are coming from."

## Tracking System

Track these every week — keep it simple, keep it consistent:

- Where are new inquiries coming from?
- Are prospects using your language when they reach out? (Words like "firefighting," "chaos," "IT manager" — if they are, your positioning is landing)
- Which specific piece of content or outreach is generating the most response?
- What objections are coming up most often? (These become your next content topics)
- How many free systems reviews are being booked versus how many convert to clients?
